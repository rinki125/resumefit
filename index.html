Under the hood

Built as a B.Tech mini project with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no backend.

score = 0.65 × skill coverage + 0.35 × cosine similarity cosine(A, B) = (A · B) / (‖A‖ × ‖B‖) on TF-IDF vectors
How is the text compared?

Both documents are cleaned, split into words and stripped of stop words. Each word gets a TF-IDF weight, then the cosine of the angle between the two vectors shows how similar they are.

How are skills found?

A dictionary of 50+ skills with aliases (for example “js” for JavaScript) is matched against both texts. Skills in the job description but not in the resume are your gaps.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to any server. PDF reading loads the open-source pdf.js library from a CDN.

What are the limits?

Scanned PDFs (images) can’t be read, so paste the text instead. Skill detection only knows the built-in list, so very niche skills won’t be flagged.
